UMWA LOCAL 8982

Install the UMWA 8982 App

Put Local 8982 directly on your phone for quick access to meeting information, union resources and optional Local notifications.

iPhone / iOS

Install on iPhone

1

Open Safari

Open Safari on your iPhone and go to umwa8982.org.

2

Tap Share

Tap the Safari Share button, then scroll through the available actions.

3

Add to Home Screen

Select Add to Home Screen.

4

Open as Web App

Make sure Open as Web App is enabled when that option appears, then tap Add.

5

Open UMWA 8982

The UMWA 8982 icon will appear on your Home Screen. Tap the icon to launch the app.

Android

Install on Android

1

Open Chrome

Open Google Chrome on your Android phone and go to umwa8982.org.

2

Open the Chrome Menu

Tap the three-dot menu in the upper-right corner of Chrome.

3

Install the App

Choose Install app or Add to Home screen, depending on the version of Chrome on your phone.

4

Confirm

Follow Chrome's confirmation prompt to add UMWA 8982 to your phone.

5

Open UMWA 8982

Tap the UMWA 8982 icon from your Home Screen or app list.

OPTIONAL

Turn On Local 8982 Notifications

After installing the app, open UMWA 8982. The installed app will display the Local 8982 Notifications section.

Tap Enable Notifications and allow notifications when your phone asks for permission.

Members who opt in can receive union meeting reminders and important Local 8982 announcements.
